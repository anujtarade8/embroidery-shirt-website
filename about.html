Our Journey

Founded in 2024, our company has been at the forefront of the embroidery industry, delivering exceptional quality and customer satisfaction. We started making embroidery designs for our own family members and have since expanded to serve a growing customer base.

Begin in a country like India, we always struggled with the challenges of starting a business in a competitive market. But with this new age of technology, we've been able to streamline our processes and deliver even better results.

We are committed to sustainability and ethical practices, ensuring that our products are made with care for both people and the environment.

So when you're ordering from us, you are not only buying a piece of clothing but you are supporting a dream that started in our home, and now it has become a reality. Also every shirt we make and deliver shares a story of passion and dedication, which makes every product special.
