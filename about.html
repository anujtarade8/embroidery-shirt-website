About Us

Welcome to Thread & Stitch. We specialize in creating beautiful custom embroidered designs for shirts and other garments.

Our skilled artisans are dedicated to providing high-quality embroidery services that combine creativity, comfort and style.

Whether you're looking for a unique gift or want to add a personal touch to your wardrobe, we've got you covered.

Our Journey

Founded in 2024, Thread & Stitch started with a simple idea: turning ordinary clothing into something personal and artistic.

We started making embroidery designs for our own family members and gradually developed a collection of unique embroidered shirts.

Our website makes it easier for customers to explore our collection, add products to their cart and place an order.

Our Mission

Our mission is to combine traditional embroidery with modern fashion and technology.
